7. **Negative candidates:** no default star=hope; no established theological identity for the girl; no real waking gunshot; no deathless dream answer; no automatic psychological costume/face recipe. These are pending adaptation boundaries, not automatically formal bans.
8. **Open:** unknown star trigger, certainty versus self-centeredness, and the cinematic emphasis of door/sleeve carriers remain choices for the user.

Learning/thinking, absurdity, irrelevance and withdrawal are not collapsed into “he fully understands nihilism and therefore kills himself.” R3D-A's limits remain explicit: a complete philosophical system is too strong; helplessness as the central cause remains OPEN; sympathy, shame and love of old Earth are retained.
