# Choosing movement methods

Use these methods when a particular physical or expressive uncertainty matters to the shot. They are questions for the Host and the existing professional owners, not a required sequence or an additional production gate.

## Diagnose before adding instructions

Separate an unclear human task from a control problem. If the action lacks a purpose, resistance or legible consequence, revisit its dramatic and physical design. If a plausible grip is unreachable, investigate scale, joint reach, control ownership or the rig. If a good design disappears in the submitted prompt, repair its projection or compilation. More adjectives cannot repair an unreachable hand; a new rig cannot supply an actor's intention.

When technique is uncertain, examine a relevant movement reference: comparable tool, load, grip, working height, skill and camera view. Observe support changes, force direction, contact transitions and recovery. Transfer the relationships that fit the character; do not copy another performer's exact rhythm by default. A still image can establish a grip or posture but cannot establish a force or timing sequence. Distinguish observed evidence from a proposed solution and state which references were actually viewed.

## Bodies, tools and contact

Identify what leads each relationship. A hand-led light object, a body following a heavy object and two hands sharing one tool need different control arrangements. Use one coherent trajectory authority for a shared object; independent controls should not fight over its position.

Describe contact in terms of permitted relative motion. A palm may retain a tool-local grip while the tool slides along a work surface. A foot may plant, roll around a contact point, then lift. Holding a handle need not freeze the wrist, elbow, shoulder or entire body. Check contact surfaces and orientation rather than only coincident object origins.

For a controlled handoff, establish receiving support before the outgoing grip releases. For a throw or deliberate drop, release initiates a flight phase; do not invent permanent support to satisfy a general rule. Contact, sliding, rolling, release and recontact are distinct physical states. Select the states the action actually contains.

Consider support and effort at the scale of the task. A heavy lift may require a stance change and whole-body contribution; a light adjustment may be led locally. Coordination can be simultaneous or sequential. Avoid prescribing hips-first, eyes-first, fixed overlap or identical delays for every person and action.

Use the external Blender rigging capability if skeletal reach, IK/FK, joint behavior or deformation prevents testing the intended movement. Simple spatial proxies do not automatically need an anatomical rig. Keep implementation in the external skills and shot artifacts, not a second Plugin animation system.

## Rhythm and performance

Choose pose blocking, layered motion or dense sampling for the uncertainty being tested. Do not mistake a sampling method for a creative rhythm. A tutorial's frame counts and exaggerated readability thresholds are examples under its own duration, framing and animation style.

A held pose may be an active choice; it does not require perpetual breathing noise or small gestures. Repetition may communicate skilled labor without a new dramatic change on each cycle. A forceful controlled action may be fast and large. The character's task, load, attention and relationship determine whether an accent, interruption, adjustment or stillness is useful.

Review the action at playback speed and through its critical transitions. Ask what the audience can read, whether support and contact remain plausible, and whether motion expresses the adopted performance. Trajectory reports and frame samples locate problems but do not certify acting. Static support-polygon checks do not judge running or intentional falling. A geometric volume centroid is not a measured human center of mass.

## Preserve the existing handoff

Put causal action and relevant contact states in the existing Action Bible / action phase and physical-state fields. Keep dramatic intention and performance axes in their existing performance owner. Do not invent a new phase, event or document just to satisfy this guide.

For Blender, transmit relevant geometry, timing, scale, grip and allowed motion along with the adopted source pins. For the final video prompt, express the visible action, resistance and consequence in ordinary language; IK names and control implementation are usually irrelevant. Formal images retain identity and appearance; an adopted preview video carries its declared spatial, motion and timing reference role. Verify the actual compiled request rather than assuming the link to a source document transmits its contents.

## Sources and limits

These are independently worded selection notes, not a redistribution of the external skill packages. The repositories were inspected at the following commits on 2026-10-19. Repository explanations are secondary material: linked expert videos were not watched in this review.

- [Scenario Blender skills, ccc6c5c623a4f96423df0d157b8ef174f14edfd8](https://github.com/scenario-labs/skills/tree/ccc6c5c623a4f96423df0d157b8ef174f14edfd8/skills/dcc/blender): animation expert notes, procedures, critique and scripts are useful for selecting and measuring methods. In particular, contact control, contact pivots, camera-space measurement and reach/support checks help diagnose execution. Use the installed expert, blockout and animation skills; read rigging when skeletal controls are needed. The repository has an MIT license. Fixed snap lengths, staging floors, beat counts and pedagogical pose order are not general live-action requirements.
- [creativedswork/animation-skills, e5411ffd51003af138022e32fb97392218b01043](https://github.com/creativedswork/animation-skills/tree/e5411ffd51003af138022e32fb97392218b01043/.trae/skills): character-body-mechanics, character-prop-interaction and character-acting provide useful distinctions for support transitions, leader/follower relationships, local grip points and task-based performance. Their gait, sit/stand, lifting, tool-impact and performance references were reviewed. The source labels these as knowledge drafts awaiting production evaluation; they are not validated motion assets. No repository license was found in the inspected tree. No package or scripts are copied into the Plugin.
- [snowfrost/skill-movie, 69f6db670ecf305e84f199e418bddbf2c1d988fe](https://github.com/snowfrost/skill-movie/tree/69f6db670ecf305e84f199e418bddbf2c1d988fe/action-choreography-reference): contact-constraint-topology and action-prompt-grammar-from-motion supply useful distinctions between contact freedoms and visible force/consequence.
