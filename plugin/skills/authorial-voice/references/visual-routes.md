# Authorial voice across visual routes

The literary gate, three-voice distinction, exact provenance and scarcity ledger are route-independent. CG is no reason to add an inscription. Candidate alternatives remain alternatives, not ledger uses. If both versions of a work reuse the same underlying literary decision, retain its identity/provenance; two designs alone do not count as two adopted interventions.

Presentation may differ: silence can hold designed negative space and a continuing material action; screen text can use calm legibility without effects; inscription-like coda can describe a literary form without inventing a tomb/monument inside the story; historian comment must retain its external speaking position; visual coda must add a defensible final relation rather than a character showcase.

A neutral rendered image or a dramatic silhouette does not itself earn text. Re-run the same sufficiency/over-explanation judgment against the actual version when a later edit changes the World Voice evidence. CG must preserve the screenplay's literary intent and identity/era boundaries, not make a narrator sound like a quest announcer.

Use a route-sensitive presentation sidecar under the [visual route contract](../../../docs/visual-route-contract.md); do not change LiteraryCandidate or AuthorialInterventionGate fingerprints just to add route metadata. The sidecar cannot overturn NO_AUTHORIAL_INTERVENTION or approve text. Cinematic-finishing still owns actual text display, silence and timing after adoption.
