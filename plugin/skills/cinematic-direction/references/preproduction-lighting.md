# Camera and lighting projection compatibility

Camera and Lighting are independently authored departments. Consume the
[Camera Bible](../../cinematography/SKILL.md) and
[Lighting Bible](../../lighting-design/SKILL.md) after Performance and Blocking.
The legacy LightingScript and CinematicShotSpec remain valid representations;
this projection owner never creates missing camera/light values.

Match source pins, scene/shot scope, motivated source IDs, direction, contrast,
visibility priorities, practicals and continuity against the originals. Carry
Camera perspective, height/distance, lens intention, movement and axis without
reinterpreting them. A functional camera zone permits a position, not a creative
mandate. Preserve DPD, source dialogue and SourceSoundIntent/native priority.

Return contradictions to the registered owner. Unknown visual quality remains
UNKNOWN; text validation does not constitute lookdev approval or production.
