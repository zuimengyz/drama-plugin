# Route-sensitive cinematic projection

Use the [route binding contract](../../../docs/visual-route-contract.md) alongside the existing director IR. Dramatic objective, canonical lines and DPD stay shared; realization can differ. Keep a frozen old CinematicShotSpec intact and bind a new proposal externally, never reinterpret it in place.

For stylized cinematic CG, choose readable planes and silhouette at the intended shot size before subtle facial detailing. A closer camera is useful when eyes/mouth carry information; a medium two-shot is useful when a handoff, rank relation or available path carries it. Do not universally exaggerate every expression or reject all microacting. Test readability against the actual rig/render or generated evidence when authorized.

Hero entrance: establish who occupies usable space and who waits for a decision. Show the action that creates privilege before an optional reveal; low camera is conditional, not proof of rank. Battle pressure: establish routes and contact, follow force into body/ground/formation consequence, preserve a spatial anchor. No free camera flight merely because it is possible.

Dialogue: physical baseline → received information → selected gaze/head/torso/hand change → hold of consequence. The listener retains an active task. Hold duration follows perception and choice, not a universal second count. Never synchronize eyelid, brow, nod, torso and hand for every line. Silence requires readable attention and a world continuing around the decision, not an idle loop.

Restrained tragedy/anger/heroism: preserve volume and identity through deformation; allow asymmetric selected movement and clear weight shifts. Grief can change a reach or interrupted task without tears or a giant collapse. Authority can be enacted by ending negotiation, letting others approach, or choosing where to stand. Do not map all rulers to fists or all grief to bowed heads.

Anti-showcase: reject unmotivated 360 orbits, endless slow motion, recurrent activation poses and edits without new information or consequence. Cut on discovery, altered intention, contact or response. Cloth, dust and hair follow causes; no permanent dramatic wind.

Visual-route grammar is separate from provider/input mode. The dry-run route sidecar is not a cinematic freeze or a paid production request. Existing downstream qualification and approval duties still apply.
