# Literary craft: choose a speaker and an action before a register

Use for dialogue revision, long speeches, in-scene documents and closure comparisons.
A form is a speaking relationship, not a vocabulary upgrade. Record a short Literary
Decision Trace: source passage → speaker/recipient/authority → immediate action →
form/register → alternatives rejected → what the listener or world changes → provenance.

## Three voices and their owners

Character Voice attempts something on a listener: command, delay, bargain, accuse,
confess, distract, persuade. World Voice is what actions, objects, space and sound
already disclose. Authorial Voice changes the work's relation to its audience after
these voices have reached their limit. The first two belong here; route an earned
closure proposal to authorial-voice, with silence as a complete competing choice.
An in-scene letter, memorial or proclamation is not authorial narration merely
because it sounds literary. Identify who wrote it, who has it and who may hear it.

## Choose form through a real use

- Historian comment separates a subject's explanation from the record of actions.
  Learn from the final comment in [Shiji 7](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/史記/卷007):
  recognition of achievement need not accept the protagonist's account of failure.
  A later comment cannot silently become a character's self-knowledge.
- An inscription or tomb notice preserves a particular life. In
  [Han Hua's burial notice](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/寒花葬志), ordinary remembered
  actions carry affection. Do not fabricate such memories for an undeveloped extra.
- A ritual address has a relationship and an addressee. The doubts and practical
  arrangements in [Han Yu's address](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/祭十二郎文) are
  emotional structure, not interruptions to a beautiful lament.
- A memorial petitions upward under risk. [Li Mi's petition](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/陳情表_(西晉))
  turns shared values and competing obligations into a request. Preserve the decision
  being sought; do not give every low-ranking speaker a formal petition register.
- A proclamation recruits and threatens. [Luo Binwang's text](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hans/討武檄)
  shows authority claims, force, rewards and consequences. Its accusations are partisan
  speech, not independently established narrator facts.
- A fu can stage opposed ways of seeing. In [the first Red Cliff fu](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/前赤壁賦),
  sensory events lead to disputation and a changed relation. A long speech without
  an opponent, resistance or altered stakes is not rescued by philosophical diction.
- Narrative poetry can let changed company or silence carry the ending; see
  [Shihao official](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/石壕吏). Study the distribution of
  speech and omission, not the insertion of a poem over every suffering image.

These later texts are comparative methods, not vocabulary licenses for earlier eras.
For actual historical quotation pin edition, exact excerpt and speaker attribution.
Separate ORIGINAL_PROJECT_TEXT from ADAPTED_HISTORICAL_TEXT and HISTORICAL_QUOTE.
Do not borrow a translated/adapted line and label it verbatim history.

## Identity, language and pressure

Differentiate what people can know, promise, command and risk, not education as a
single high/low slider. A boatman can give expert operational instructions; a ruler
can ask a plain practical question. A scholar may use a short threat when time is
short. Test exchanging speakers: does the tactic or authority become false?

Vary 文白比例 through action. Retain a sourced formal passage when a public claim,
ritual or deliberate self-presentation earns it; let a practical answer remain plain.
Short sentences can close options with greater force than ornamental parallelism.
Avoid automatic deletion of all long passages or automatic classicization of all
characters. Do not imitate antique grammar by scattering 兮、矣、哉 into modern syntax.
Remove ornamental diction and read aloud: if the action becomes clearer with no loss
of character, keep the plain version. If removing a formal structure loses a claim,
promise, concession or mounting argument, preserve that structure and clarify it.

## Long speech as a contest

Track claim → evidence → demand → listener resistance → changed tactic. Break or
continue speech where attention, knowledge or physical opportunity changes. A repeated
sentence must exert different pressure the second time. The listener retains a job,
body and risk, not just reaction shots. Don't force a fixed line/word limit; don't
cover a redundant lecture with invented busywork. If a speech states fate the speaker
cannot know, restore uncertainty or identify it as their belief.

## Film adaptation and silence

An in-world text may be dictated, revised, delivered, withheld or contradicted by its
reception. Choose this action before designing typography or voice-over. An authorial
text should not repair a missing handoff, spatial relation or absent listener.
Compare the exact same passage with and without text. Name what words add that action
does not, what ambiguity they remove, and whether the removal is wanted. Silence is
stronger only when the relevant work has actually been done; it is not a prestige rule.

Validation needs actual choices on at least two different uses when claiming reusable
literary improvement: a closure comparison and a scene-language decision. Retain the
rejected alternative and its concrete failure. Successful examples demonstrate bounded
method use, not a universal literary-quality score or audience test.
