# Meta dramaturgy: select and derive, do not prescribe

Before a new work, judge the request and evidence. Keep these choices together under
direction.meta; revisit only if scope, evidence or creative intent changes.

## Load, scope and style

Use LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH/VERY_HIGH/N/A for historicalSpan, ensembleSize,
politicalComplexity, militaryScale, spatialComplexity, evidenceUncertainty,
informationAsymmetry, emotionalIntensity, continuityHorizon, spectacleRequirement,
dialogueDensity and subjectiveIntensity. Give a short rationale; no arithmetic score.
These priorities activate existing capabilities, not required scenes or word counts.

Core: historical grounding, causality, time/space, character, relationship/power,
knowledge, continuity, pacing, dialogue/subtext and review. A silent scene still gets
a dialogue applicability decision. Conditional: war/army, large ensemble, siege
logistics, parallel locations, spectacle, document/artifact narrative. Set each
conditional use to N/A, BASE or FOCUS with a reason. N/A never demands an insertion.

narrativeAperture = included, excluded, startState, endState, selectionReason.
The whole history is a boundary; the selected slice is the work. Explain exclusions
that would otherwise tempt compression or digression. Derive narrativeTexture from
the chosen action, social world and audience experience. No preference for tragedy,
restraint, sorrow, silence, speed or grandeur. A vigorous argument or delighted shout
can be as apt as stillness; an explicit ending can be as apt as an unresolved one.

## POV and bounded subjectivity

povContract = primaryPOV, secondaryPOV, audiencePrivilege, allowedOmniscience,
forbiddenOmniscience, POVTransitionRule. Use speaker IDs or named perspective scopes.
Decide whether ensemble, distant institutions or brief objective exposition belongs
here; neither single-character restriction nor omniscience is the default.

subjectiveLens = anchorCharacter, emotionalFilter, audienceDistance,
allowedOmniscience, subjectiveMoments, objectiveMoments. Each subjective moment names
its scene, anchor, expression, narrative purpose and objective return. Anchor may
change under the POV transition rule. Attention, framing emphasis, breathing, sound,
silence, gestures, transitions or memory-like perception are options, not a recipe.
An objective return restores the wider stakes or spatial/causal reality when closeness
would hide them. A remembered scene must be identifiable as memory/perception, with
its event time distinct from presentation time. Do not invent inaccessible truth.

Ask whose experience this is, why the audience is close now, and what would be lost
without that closeness. Never override confirmed facts, identity, decisive causality,
known place/time or outcomes. A false perception belongs to its perceiver; the Bible's
objective facts remain unchanged. Subjective elapsed time is not new historical time.

## Elasticity and rhythm

characters[*].elasticity = normalBehavior, pressureResponse, breakCondition,
possibleDeviation, recoveryPattern. A deviation requires established pressure/body/
power/knowledge evidence and a reason; do not retrofit a new trait to excuse a twist.
Recovery may be partial, delayed or absent if supported. State which invariant remains
and what actually changes. These are bounded interpretations, not medical diagnoses.

For each episode keep episodeJob, startState, endState, tensionShape, majorTurn,
emotionalPeak, breathingSpace, afterEffect, nextEpisodePressure. Compare beat tactics,
scene delta, sequence movement, episode rhythm and the entire arc. Build, release,
breathing, reorientation, escalation, climax and aftermath belong where useful, not
once each per episode. State transitions cross episode cuts; new episodes do not
heal injuries, reset trust, erase promises or grant knowledge automatically.

## Anchors and bridges belong to writing

Each episode has openingShotAnchor and closingShotAnchor; scene/turn anchors are
optional where a specific perception matters. Describe perception, purpose and the
derivation from thesis/aperture/POV/texture/episode job. Opening asks first seen/heard,
information or feeling first, and why here. Closing asks last seen/heard, intended
knowledge/emotion and whether closure, pressure, release or another effect is earned.
No mandatory entry categories or ending device. Dialogue/text can close a work when
it acts; neither a slogan nor automatic silence substitutes for a dramatic ending.

visualEmotionalBridge links emotion → behavior → medium → emphasis → transition;
record only when it explains a meaningful change of audience attention. It is not a
fixed chain of shots. No exact shot list, camera number, lens, frame size, duration,
movement or generation parameters. Writing owns intention; shot planning owns execution.

For a document/artifact, record original/paraphrase/reconstruction, source and locator,
and factual attributes that cannot be changed. A visible original keeps its actual
wording, including errors; translated or adapted speech is labelled separately. An
object can function as evidence, emotional focus, transition or anchor, but must not
silently acquire false provenance, date, author, addressee or causal power.
