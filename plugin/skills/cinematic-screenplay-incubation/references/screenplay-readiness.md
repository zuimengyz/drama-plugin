# Screenplay readiness and upstream challenge

Before full-film direction, read the entire current Work/Script/Episode/Scenes
and bind exact pins. Produce ScreenplayReadinessReview, not an average score.
Every dimension needs PASS/NOTE/MAJOR/UNKNOWN/NA, concrete source location,
reason, and an upstream repair scope for unresolved issues:
historical causality; narrative spine; protagonist decisions; character arc;
human stakes; emotional counterline; internal/external conflict; scene variety;
scene necessity; setup/payoff; dialogue; literary opportunity; action/drama
balance; climax architecture; ending weight; audience knowledge progression.
NA needs a reason and cannot excuse core causality or human stakes.

Human stakes means a perceivable person/relationship/value that choices can hurt,
protect or irreversibly change. Comrades, trust, obligation, home, dignity,
parent/child, ruler/subject or the protagonist's relation to the absent can carry
it. Romance is never mandatory. Specify who/what carries the stakes and where it
changes action. An event → report → decision → march → battle chain without a
human carrier cannot silently pass. Relationship-rich scenes without causal
choices cannot pass either. An emotional counterline may be quiet listening,
a material obligation or a repeated task whose meaning changes; it is not a
mandatory secondary plot or sentimental speech. Repetition needs actual dramatic
progression, not renamed scenes.

Historical Dramatic Affordance Review reads opportunities in supplied evidence:
song, letter, ritual, relationship, attendant, farewell, object or custom. For
significant available opportunities record source/limits, thematic relevance and
INCLUDE / EXCLUDE_WITH_REASON / UNRESOLVED. Do not invent missing history or treat
a famous episode as compulsory. Exclusion is legitimate when aperture, causality,
POV or dramatic concentration warrants it; explain the lost expressive function
and whether another supported carrier supplies it. Unresolved significant omission
returns upstream. Authorial-voice remains separately gated; historical literature
does not authorize narrator text or extra dialogue.

Use `preproduction.readiness`: READY_FOR_DIRECTION / READY_WITH_NOTES /
DIRECTOR_REQUESTS_SCRIPT_REVIEW. This function enforces recorded evidence and
blockers; it cannot decide literary merit on its own. Director challenges precise
findings and returns to this owner before final coverage, lighting or design.
The screenplay owner may propose focused revision; only the authorized existing
source owner can update formal content after the user's screenplay review.
Never silently revise a plot inside a Director Book. Current formal content being
valid or historically disciplined does not prove it is artistically ready.
