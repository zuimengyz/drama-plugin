# Cinematic and Visual Criticism

Reuse director, blocking, cinematography, lighting-design, specialized-asset-design, scene-layout, production-design and color-design. Begin with what the frame permits the viewer to know, feel or withhold.

Trace attention and power: distance, depth, access, obstruction, gaze, hands, entrances, exits, camera relation and changing space. Ask whether the same handsome arrangement would mean anything different if the encounter changed. Beauty can deepen a world or merely decorate narrated events.

Compare intended blocking and camera decisions with the source-pinned realized sequence. A plan's close approach, unequal distance or delayed reveal is not visible success until seen. Separate generation limitation from weak artistic choice; provider diagnosis needs execution evidence.

Evaluate the world as inhabited relations: objects used, work, weather, strangers with their own purpose, material constraint and historical/social position. Do not demand more extras or invented episodes. A sparse room can be alive through one consequential object or unseen relation.

Inspect light and art for visibility, selective attention, time and material meaning. No compulsory darkness, warm/cool palette, symmetry violation, camera movement or continuity perfection. Small artifacts matter only when they break the work's key recognition, performance or relation.

Offer a rival interpretation of a sustained composition or absence before declaring it inert. Preserve formal distance and opacity when they carry meaning. Still frames cannot establish exact micro-reactions, optical motion or continuous timing; link claims to the actual observation mode.

Read [evidence and independence](../artistic-review-council/references/evidence-method.md) before judging media. Use [comparative craft sources](../artistic-review-council/references/critical-traditions.md) when invoking theory or a film tradition; distinguish the cited author's view from your inference. Read the relevant production Skill for its intent and vocabulary, without adopting its authority or self-review as proof of success.

Separate observed fact, interpretation and proposed revision. Each material judgment needs a source/version, scene-local time or text locator, the specific expressive relation, its consequence for the viewer, the strongest alternative reading and a confidence limit. Describe success as carefully as failure. Missing sound or continuous playback narrows the claim; it does not license inferred boredom, emotion or musical effect.
