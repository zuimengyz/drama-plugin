# Use film practice to widen a choice

When a passage's expressive options feel narrow, optionally consult the separate
[film-practice library](../../../knowledge/film-craft/README.md) by the narrative
problem: what must the spectator first experience, fail to notice, remember or
reconsider? Retrieve a few relevant cases, not a prescribed number per scene.
Separate documented scene facts, attributed filmmaker statements and our analysis;
do not turn a critic's reading into the director's declared intention.

Compare materially different audience experiences before selecting coverage.
An opening may establish a lived world, a task, an absence or a misunderstanding;
a memory may alter present listening without becoming a flashback. These are
options, not defaults. Develop the current work's own people, relations and form,
and name the selected gain and the meaningful loss in existing Host decisions.
Reject precedents that distort the character's agency or the work's chronology.

Departments respond to that selected experience in their own craft. When adopting
or trimming coverage, compare what the spectator can now understand and feel with
what the original encounter required. A retained prop, action or background crowd
does not by itself preserve an experience of cooperation, waiting or exclusion.
Protect a necessary experience through the existing Editorial/PictureEditPlan
decision, without treating every intended shot or hold as indispensable.

Keep case sources and alternatives in author/support context. Only the selected
current audiovisual decisions enter FilmDirection, professional originals and
final Prompt writing. Do not paste case prose, film imitation instructions or
research notes into generation text. No new loop, mandatory research, approval,
score, duration rule or automatic author call is introduced.

## Optional expression-led reading in actual author calls

Use the existing local reader, not a parallel research service:
`python plugin/knowledge/film-craft/find_cases.py --expression "希望观众逐渐发现门外的事情" --limit 6`.
Use `--style`, `--goal`, `--means`, `--discipline` to narrow a question and `--case ID --read` to read selected originals. See [style/goal navigation](../../../knowledge/cinema-styles/README.md). Known expression aliases expand to goals, not to a prescribed camera move; unknown phrases may have no match.

When useful, supply `craftKnowledge` in existing `authorContext`, screenplay context or professional context: `{ "query": "逐渐发现门外公共生活", "caseIds": ["playtime", "children-of-men"], "techniqueIds": ["camera-movement", "lighting-placement"], "limit": 6 }`. Omit the selection when no reading is useful. Director treatment and native Direction/Professional calls load the exact case/method bodies and hashes through the same local reader. This is advisory context; no fixed case count, additional author call, adoption or Gate follows.

Read before choosing. In existing author/support context retain the expression question, the useful or rejected method and its tradeoff. Then translate the selected method into this scene: camera side/height/direction, people and landmarks, trigger/path/end, world-space light direction/extent and reflected or negative fill, receiving/action time, and sound/cut relation. Unknown original-film parameters remain unknown. A fixed view or withholding attention can be the right choice.

Coordinate through existing owners. Camera owns its view/movement; Blocking/Performance own people; Lighting owns sources and quality; World/Production Design supplies and reconciles space; Editorial/Sound own cuts and listening. Put current executable choices in existing Camera `cameraPosition`, `lensIntention`, `movement` and Lighting `sources`, `directionAndQuality`, `constraints` (and neighboring Action/Sound facts). Reasons left only in `continuityInterpretation` do not execute. Case titles, director names, URLs and research prose never enter model facts as style commands. Compare the actual prepared text with the necessary choices; a successful transfer still does not prove model realization.
