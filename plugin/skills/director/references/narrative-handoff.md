# Director narrative originals before coverage

Consume the exact selected writer screenplay without replacing it. Develop a
full-film audiovisual narrative original: narrative voice, camera standpoint,
spectator knowledge, time/space, opening/ending and image/sound/edit relations.
Then develop the current scene's complete viewing and listening experience from
that selected film original, the scene screenplay and necessary neighbors.
Neither task requires a lens list, fixed duration, extra conflict or multiple proposals.
Narrating voice, camera subjectivity and spectator knowledge are independent choices.

Read selected `filmIdentity` world/look/source-analysis prose and current scene
state, plus selected structured `filmLook` when used. Establish the film's world
and positive visual proposition before coverage; unknown era/place details remain
open choices, never random completions. Scene prose begins with a compact readable
place/time/environment/look/source heading. Distinguish era, season, time of day,
elapsed time and changing light; authored exceptions need not be naturalistic.
Use [world and visual inheritance](world-visual-inheritance.md) for the existing
original binding and owner handoff. No baseline is a production adoption.

`directorNarrative.film` and `.scenes` carry selected prose and selection evidence.
The old `sceneDirection` means ordered Shot coverage; never use its existence as
proof that the scene narrative task happened. Preserve meaningful waiting,
coexistence and offscreen experience as well as dramatic change. Proposed writer
changes stay separate feedback until Host selection and existing Canon adoption.

Use expression-led case reading when useful. Choose or reject a case's method,
then express the selected relationship in current camera/actor/light/sound/edit
terms. A cited film or repeated abstract intention does not deliver the choice.
Detailed formal professional designs still require Canon + Shot. Early advice is
optional; no extra approval cycle or mandatory specialty dispatch follows.

The default native path uses Director-authored candidates for coverage, labelled
DIRECTOR_AUTHOR_CANDIDATE, not human approval. Host prose may be explicitly bound
with screenplay.bind_director_narrative. Only selected originals are loaded; no
history sweep. Existing frozen coverage is recovered without filling missing new
narrative layers. Host retains final selection and existing production adoption.
