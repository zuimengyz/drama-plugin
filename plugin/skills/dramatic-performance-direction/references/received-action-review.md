# Received action, expression and attention

Use this method where a material receiving or attention relation is missing or
ambiguous in the existing handoff. It develops existing DPD and professional originals; it
adds no contract, production Gate, approval step or generation permission. A
short scene may need only a few linked sentences. Locate the finding once in the
existing preparation/review notes; each owner addresses only its affected part,
and downstream readers reuse that location rather than repeat a four-department
audit. A correctly carried relation needs no additional record or expression.
Do not turn the questions into
mandatory fields or an equal number of expressive beats for every work.

## Locate the actor in time

Read a consequential Line as its speaker, against the immediately available
source. Which earlier word, sight, silence, prior knowledge or physical result
has reached this actor? What is the actor trying now? What answer is still
unknown? Put that actor's continuity/change in `LineDPD.changeFromPrevious` and
`continuity`. Preserve exact words, speaker and source bindings. A partner's later
choice belongs to that partner; the present speaker may expect it, not know it
has occurred. A Beat's `transitionTrigger` can legitimately specify the future
event that releases its next phase.

Separate the time represented by the words from the time of speaking. Remembered
events, offscreen/voice-over speech and imagined addressees retain their source
roles. Anticipation can precede a sentence's end when an earlier cue or established
knowledge supports it. Name that cause rather than imposing a universal rule to
wait for every full stop. Preserve selected overlap and intentional uncertainty.

## Make the encounter available to performers

Use the common DPD to author outward realization in the Performance Bible. Ask
what can be seen of giving this particular line, receiving it, or continuing a
task while refusing an answer. A change can live in the direction or persistence
of a look, a face losing its previous address, vigorous work, a withheld action,
or purposeful stillness. Choose what carries the relation at the selected scale;
do not enumerate muscles, assign an emotion to every word, or require added motion.

Give an important listener their own source-supported task and identify the
received cue, response/non-response and return or continuation. Locate concurrent
expression in the applicable current Action when it is not shared across a Beat.
First assess whether existing work, attention or stillness already makes the
relation playable. A standing pose, occupied hands or the word “restrained” alone
does not answer that question; it is not a reason to prescribe another face action.
An unchanging face may be the
appropriate realization: retain the task and consequence instead of decorating it.

Distinguish the line's addressee, a third-person referent and the direction of
attention. A gesture identifying someone does not automatically ask that person
to answer or require every witness to turn. Blocking resolves real targets and
facing; Action preserves support/contact mechanics; Voice supplies audible delivery.
Neither channel invents another objective or treats pressure as a compulsory
increase in amplitude. Use the same receiving and release event across owners,
without adding a separate pause, breath or reaction hold in each one.

## Carry attention across a boundary

Compare the selected outgoing and incoming phases, including attention, facing,
active task and held-object support. Use actual adopted media for what it shows;
use approved design for what is intended, and leave unseen/unheard details unknown.
A head direction alone cannot establish eye fixation or emotional meaning.

For continuous time, locate an attention transfer needed for the next address on
one side of the cut, with a source-supported cue and matching state on the other.
An audible address may turn a listener during the line. Do not reset both actors
at the cut or repeat a completed transfer at a technical file boundary. If the
selected edit permits an omitted/offscreen interval, record that relation rather
than manufacture an obligatory visible turn. Deliberate discontinuity remains
available under the approved film intent; continuity is not a demand for seamlessness.

## Read what the next owner actually gets

Read the execution excerpt or final projection in scene order, without relying
on unprojected explanatory notes. Can its current subject perform this action
from what has reached them? Can the listener continue their own task? Does the
boundary preserve or explain attention, and do body and voice share the relevant
cue and release? Trace a material omission or premature consequence to the owner
and exact source/line/phase; revise that original and inspect the projection again.
Do not copy the whole DPD into execution text or create a parallel action track.

A useful check states the particular role/time relation, selected visible/audible
consequence, its surviving location in the handoff and any unresolved evidence.
The record can live in the existing preparation/review notes. Structural validation
checks identities and fingerprints; semantic reading checks the authored relation;
actual media review checks whether the relation is realized. Keep those claims
separate, including when the original guidance already produces a good result.

## Applicability and counterexample

This is most useful when receiving, withholding or redirecting attention changes
what a partner can do. Ritual, montage, observational passages, deliberate opacity
and a deliberately unreadable face may organize meaning differently. Do not force
them into a reaction for each line or explain away a selected gap. Revisit or
withdraw the method where its bookkeeping adds no playable relation, overstates
knowledge, homogenizes faces/timing or burdens a downstream excerpt with psychology.
One successful text comparison supports a local method, not a general artistic
law or a claim of improved film performance.
