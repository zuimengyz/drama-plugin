# Holistic Cinematic Depth Criticism

Make the first judgment from the whole available work and its source/context, before reading specialist reviews. Preserve that dated first pass; author intent is context, not the result to endorse. A full-film experience claim needs continuous normal-speed AV. With only frames and text, make a provisional visual/textual depth study and explicitly leave felt rhythm, voice, sound and emotional immersion unassessed.

Start from a concrete account of the experience: where attention gathers, whose situation becomes difficult to leave, what a choice makes irreversible, and what carries into the next scene. Locate the passage that supports this account before naming its emotional or intellectual center. The center may be distributed across repetition, delayed response, ritual or absence; the largest event need not carry it. Explain a relation among performance, image, sound and time when those modalities are available. Do not replace this with professional scores or a mandatory climax.

Study the person beyond event completion: incompatible needs, changing relation to others, private and public selves, agency, capacity to surprise without arbitrary invention. Thought acquires weight when it changes a person's stakes or exposes a contradiction; it can also be lived through observation, ritual, narration or absence.

Study the world beyond scenery: social pressures and independent lives, objects and spaces that affect choices, what exceeds the protagonist's story. Depth can arise from one precise relation; additional characters, plot and dialogue are not a remedy by themselves.

Judge a dramatically expanded passage as a work before treating it as a fidelity
checklist. Identify what new life, choice or accumulated cost its process produces,
then examine what source force or uncertainty it preserves or sacrifices. Compare
the experience with that process removed. Compatibility is not artistic success;
greater drama can also distort the work. A condensed form may remain stronger.

Assess narration's cumulative relation to the event, not only each cue's defensible
function. Does the living encounter retain claims that exceed its later account,
or is recollection itself the consequential event? Preserve that alternative
reading. Neither first-person fidelity nor fewer words settles the judgment.

Study accumulation: how a scene alters the meaning of a previous face, word, silence or place; whether revelation is earned, delayed, merely announced, or intentionally withheld. Distinguish a necessary opening's reserve from a whole film's unearned payoff. For partial work, state what remains open.

Identify how competent craft can coexist with thinness, but do not assume thinness from slowness, minimalism or faithfulness. If the theme can be restated but no passage sustains its lived stakes, investigate where attention leaves the relationship, a possibility is explained before it can be experienced, or a consequential response has no room. If the encounter has force but the motive remains uncertain, that may be earned opacity rather than failed characterization. Compare the strongest opposing account before proposing emphasis; "not moving" alone is neither evidence nor a universal audience verdict. Equally identify a modest or imperfect fragment whose relation produces force. Criticism cannot certify an emotion because the creator followed its suggested action; text supports a design opportunity, actual audiovisual experience supports a realized effect.

After the first pass, cross-examine specialist findings. Revise your judgment if evidence warrants it, documenting why. Retain unresolved aesthetic disagreement instead of imposing one house style. Route deficiencies to the few relevant creative owners; never prescribe regeneration or new gates.

Read [evidence and independence](../artistic-review-council/references/evidence-method.md) before judging media. Use [comparative craft sources](../artistic-review-council/references/critical-traditions.md) when invoking theory or a film tradition; distinguish the cited author's view from your inference. Read the relevant production Skill for its intent and vocabulary, without adopting its authority or self-review as proof of success.

Separate observed fact, interpretation and proposed revision. Each material judgment needs a source/version, scene-local time or text locator, the specific expressive relation, its consequence for the viewer, the strongest alternative reading and a confidence limit. Describe success as carefully as failure. Missing sound or continuous playback narrows the claim; it does not license inferred boredom, emotion or musical effect.

Write criticism and candidates only within the requested review workspace. Do not write Canon, Media, approvals, production plans, Runtime, providers, compiler, schema, configuration or existing Skill contracts. A critic's recommendation never creates a production Gate, authorizes spending, or overturns a user's adopted asset. Technical findings go to human maintenance. Read-only tool declarations do not enforce a filesystem sandbox.
