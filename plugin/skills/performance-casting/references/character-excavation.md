# Character excavation that survives contact with identity and era

Ask why the viewer would keep watching this person. Beauty, danger and dignity are
possible answers, not universal settings. Identify the role's habitual decisions:
who reports to them, who can refuse them, what they may give or withhold, when they
must ask, and what a concession costs in this world. This changes stance, gaze,
listening, interruption, closeness and the handling of humiliation. Warmth from a
ruler can be a deliberate reduction of distance; warmth from a peer has other stakes.

Use CharacterExcavation within the existing RoleArchetypeProfile. Ten linked insights
cover personality; identity/rank/occupation; social position; cultural/historical
environment; external perception; strength–fatality unity; character–fate contrast;
counter-stereotype; performance range; and visual casting implications. Each names
its evidence class, actual source refs and at least one decision with an observable
test in FACE, SCALE, SOCIAL or PERFORMANCE. No extra prose quota. A nontragic role
may identify a cost or explicitly reject an inevitable fatal flaw.

IdentityEraContext retains only consequential rank, occupation, class/social position,
institutional power, situated values, habitual decision rights and expected reactions.
Unranked or unempowered is a legitimate authored value. Children and ordinary people
still occupy relations; they need no artificial sovereign authority.

Sanity checks are interpretive reviews, not word searches:

- IDENTITY_LOST: remove the proper name and read the account. Can one still identify
  the type of work, rank, dependence, social rights and other people's treatment?
  FAIL if only bravery, insecurity, pride or vulnerability remains.
- ERA_CONTEXT_LOST: does the explanation preserve the world's actual obligations,
  honor/shame, material possibilities and power? FAIL if a historical conflict has
  silently become modern therapeutic self-realization. Modern settings also need
  their own institutions and constraints; universal human feeling is not forbidden.

Each PASS/FAIL includes reasoning tied to the excavation fingerprint. The gate
detects failed/missing/stale review; it does not establish that a Host's interpretation
is historically correct. Source claims require sources; an opponent's appraisal is
not a neutral personality diagnosis. Keep counterevidence instead of constructing
a uniformly flattering or damning character. Do not infer intelligence from political
success/failure, or real personality from physical face structure.

Strength–fatality unity asks whether the same resource both works and becomes costly
under a different condition. It does not prove an inevitable destiny or a diagnosis.
Fate contrast asks what must remain available before the outcome: vitality before
defeat, trust before betrayal, humor before loss. Do not remove the baseline merely
because the ending is known. Counter-stereotype must preserve defining role powers:
making a warrior warm should not erase martial capacity; making a ruler direct
should not make every subordinate an equal friend.

SocialPresenceProof compares relations, not entourage decoration. State participants,
shared space, camera controls, decision rights, visible attention/spacing, success
observations and invalidating cheats. A ferry professional may guide a king's feet
while respecting his rank; a counselor can command a hearing without owning the
final decision. Avoid kneeling crowds, center-position posters, colored rank coding,
or giving the lead magnificent costume and everyone else rags. For ordinary group
membership, being plausibly included can be the success criterion.

Changing interpretation requires a new profile and an explicit rereview of retained
images, never a rewritten old review. State what those older test conditions cannot
show. A newly authorized narrowed search is a documented continuation, not a technical
retry; preserve task IDs, spend to date and former decisions.
