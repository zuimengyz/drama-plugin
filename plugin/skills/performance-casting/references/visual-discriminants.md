# Appeal and discriminants that reach the image request

AudienceAppealMode describes the attraction appropriate to this role, with ordered
channels linked to visual/proof choices. Refined, heroic, regal, martial, commanding,
dangerous, charismatic, gentle, intellectual and tragic appeal are examples; custom
modes are allowed. A ferry worker's competence, a child's persistence or a ruler's
accessibility need not resemble a warrior's force. RESPECT means an audience response,
not approval of coercion. Never infer real character or rank from anatomy.

Being liked and commanding respect can coexist. Decide their priority from role intent.
Search must admit distinctive, imperfect possibilities; selection may reject them.
Do not make every search image conventionally handsome before testing individuality.
Keep meaningful culture, identity and user boundaries, not a universal avoidance checklist.

Author a VisualCastingPlan against the current RoleArchetypeProfile fingerprint. Its
CastingIntent resolves actual excavation, identity/era/social context, counter-stereotype
and scene-obligation JSON pointers. Each appeal channel must affect named discriminants.
A discriminant declares source pointers, axis, alternatives, observation and responsibility:

- DIRECTLY_VISUALIZABLE: face or body structures. Authored craniofacial choices are
  screen design, not physiognomy. Describe positions/relationships, not just 'unique'.
- BODY_SPATIAL_ONLY: relative scale, posture and social relations; not face evidence.
- PERFORMANCE_DEPENDENT: role-specific changes/behavior; not fixed anatomy.
- STORY_DEPENDENT: decisions, political limitations, generosity's cost, fate and shame;
  retain for direction, never use as static-face rejection criteria.

A generosity scene can have observable performance, but one still cannot prove generosity
or political intelligence. Tragic appeal means a capacity to travel an arc, not sad eyes.

Contrastive variants choose different options on authored face axes. Distinct option IDs
and wording are necessary but not sufficient: the Host must explain why the structures
cannot all describe the same face. 'Strong', 'more commanding' and another seed do not
establish contrast. The contract checks categorical separation; it cannot prove semantic
exclusion or model adherence. Keep this limitation in reviews.

CLI compile emits four visual buckets plus story exclusions, exact prompt, provenance
and prompt fingerprint. Only the chosen stage's visible instructions enter generation;
interpretation stays in trace. Appeal appears only through relevant authored audience
instructions, not the entire biography. check-projection requires the actual submitted
text to equal the compiled text. Verify the returned execution graph too. Provider
internal optimization remains unknown unless it is exposed.

For calibration use purpose CALIBRATION and explicit identical base conditions. It
omits role appeal from the prompt, varies declared geometry only, and cannot produce a
selection finalist. If optimizer controls exist, a matched geometry × optimizer comparison
can test responsiveness. Keep seed/controls fixed where supported, quotes current and
samples minimal. One image per cell is a diagnostic observation, not a statistically
isolated cause; no automatic retries for artistic preference or automatic model changes.

CompleteCandidateProofSet requires FACE, BODY/SCALE and SOCIAL for COMPLETE scope.
Scale need not be exceptional: ordinary proportions/posture can be the claim. Performance
contrast is required when role obligations depend on range. Reduced roles must explain
omissions and cannot call that reduced scope a complete proof. For leaders, commanders,
counselors with pivotal influence and people holding local professional power, explicitly
consider social identity; if essential, SOCIAL cannot be omitted. Avoid entourage-as-ruler
shortcuts. Others' attention, spacing and decision rights must be observable.

Use AudienceAppealFinding with the current plan fingerprint and authored LIKING/RESPECT/
MIXED response. In visual_selection_gate it is essential, not a numerical bonus that beauty
can compensate for. Keep limitations by stage. Every candidate remains PENDING_USER_REVIEW;
complete proof permits user selection only. Existing production design and freeze retain
formal appearance/production authority; no compiler or review may approve the character.
