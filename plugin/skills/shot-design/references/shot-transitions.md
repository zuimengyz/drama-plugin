# Intended shot transitions

Extend existing EditorialRhythmPlan.transitions with ShotTransition; no new
ShotTransitionPlan truth or business entity. Each adjacent pair carries from/to
shot, end/open state, cut trigger/type, screen direction, axis, eyeline, motion,
composition relationship, sound carry/intention, time, space and why it exists.
Use hard cut, consequence, reaction, spatial reveal, match action, sound bridge,
time ellipsis or scene transition when motivated; types remain natural language.

Time must distinguish continuous, compressed continuous, explicit ellipsis, new
time and parallel. Sound may carry, cut, fade, prelap or continue offscreen.
A later injury cannot appear instantaneously through an undeclared match cut.
Maintain movement vectors/eyeline in battle and multi-person dialogue; a deliberate
axis change needs visible reorientation. Match action preserves phase and contact;
sound bridges must not falsely imply simultaneous or adjacent spaces.

Validate adjacent coverage edges with preproduction.transition_completeness.
Cross-scene transitions use the existing multi-scene EditorialRhythmPlan, with
source fingerprint of the pinned episode/sequence; include the scene boundary
receivers in its coverage. Single-view scenes need an omission reason and still
need entry/exit transitions at the episode boundary. Check all seven dimensions:
screen direction / axis / eyeline / motion / sound / time / space.

Shot-design owns intended production continuity; cinematic-finishing owns actual
cut decisions, measured timing, audio edits and PictureEditPlan after material
exists. Intended sound carry is never an executed audio edit. If material fails,
return to coverage intent or propose an actual edit through the proper owner.
