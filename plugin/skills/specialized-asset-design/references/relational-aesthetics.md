# Relational asset aesthetics

Use this knowledge in asset-plan authorship and reference artwork realization.
It supplies questions and practical methods, not a required look or a second
approval process. The selected screenplay, identity and Film Look remain owners.

Evaluate the actual accepted image before changing its design. Distinguish an
identity/construction problem from a view, pose, lighting or translation problem.
Retain successful properties; revise one causally connected problem group.

For a character, design the visible whole: hair around the face, collar joining
neck and shoulders, garment weight and fit, hands and supported posture. Select
the features that read at the proof's distance. A close face proof and a full-body
costume proof need different detail. Age, labor and wear require source support;
neither beautification nor exaggerated decay is a default.

For a person in a place, identify an observable attention object and a purposeful
hand/body relation when the proof needs performance. An identity proof may look
at the camera and stand still. Gaze direction alone does not establish emotion.
A still shows one coherent instant, not a sequence of preceding/future actions.
Do not turn an identity proof into an invented movie scene. Joint situation
proofs use the existing frame/reference route with separately selected identities
and location; they do not replace either baseline.

Give each important color a physical carrier. Describe how skin, coarse linen,
wool, worn wood, plaster or metal respond differently under shared illumination.
Use contact shadows, reflected color, scale and connected floor/support surfaces
to place people in the space. Choose light sources and attention hierarchy for
this image; no universal light count, contrast level, palette or subject ratio.

Separate durable identity, costume, construction and material from temporary
pose, crop, lighting and environment. State which attributes each actual reference
supplies and which the current proof decides. Learning examples are knowledge,
not uploaded image references, unless explicitly selected for that purpose.

Use existing design leaves and typed image facts. Put view/crop/attention in
camera, light in lighting, appearance in subject/environment, and only necessary
cross-object relationships in preserve. Do not broadcast a paragraph across
fields. Keep rationale, provenance and workflow outside executable prose.
Use concrete positive facts; add a limitation only for an observed or specific
ambiguity. MJ parameters, fixed word counts, fictional camera guarantees and
mandatory negative lists do not transfer to other image providers.

Review actual output at thumbnail, medium and detail scales: visual hierarchy;
head/hair/collar/shoulder coherence; recognizable identity; hand and support
relations; common illumination; material distinctions. Compare with the accepted
parent. Successful text compilation is not evidence of improved image quality.
