# Episode Writing

Load the episode, surrounding story, relevant entities, and evidence. Preserve each scene's dramatic objective and ensure the episode changes story state. Use concise dialogue suited to short-form pacing.

Skip already approved material, refresh context after external changes, and stop when the episode has a clear opening state, escalation, turn, exit hook, and no unresolved factual claim presented as certain.
