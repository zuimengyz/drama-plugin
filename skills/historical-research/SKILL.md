# Historical Research

Frame a focused historical question, search the most relevant evidence domain, and retain source identity with every claim. Distinguish documented fact, supported inference, and dramatic invention.

Prefer primary evidence when available. Use `history.verify_claim` for consequential assertions. Stop when the requested claims have evidence or are clearly marked unresolved; skip searches already satisfied by supplied evidence. Never invent a citation.
