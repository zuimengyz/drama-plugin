# Scene Breakdown

For each necessary scene, state the dramatic purpose, entering state, conflict, turn, exiting state, location, characters, props, and historical constraints. Consolidate scenes that do not change story state.

Use asset lookup only to identify available references, not to derive asset hierarchy locally. Completion means every retained scene earns its place and exposes enough information for visual planning.
