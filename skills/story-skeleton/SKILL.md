# Story Skeleton

Build the smallest coherent dramatic spine: premise, protagonist objective, opposing force, escalation, turning point, climax, and ending. Load story and historical context before resolving conflicts that depend on facts.

Treat the sequence as guidance, not a mandatory workflow. Reuse completed beats, revisit weak assumptions, and stop once each major beat has a clear causal role and respects stated historical constraints.
