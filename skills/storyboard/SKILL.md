# Storyboard

Map the scene's dramatic turn into the fewest shots that communicate it. For each shot, specify subject, action, framing, camera behavior, duration, transition, and effective asset references.

The ordering is advisory: reuse approved shots, revise discontinuities, or stop early when coverage is sufficient. Do not compile provider-specific generation workflows.
